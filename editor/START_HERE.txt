RB2 GALAXY STUDIO 1.0
English interface - portable offline browser app for Windows

PREDOMINANTLY AI-DEVELOPED AND AI-RESEARCHED
AI generated much of the implementation, analysis and documentation, with human
direction, game material, gameplay tests and corrections. AI output can be wrong.
See README_EN.md and the repository's evidence/confidence levels.

1. Extract this entire ZIP to a folder.
2. Double-click "Launch Galaxy Studio.cmd".
   Or open "index.html" in a current Microsoft Edge / Google Chrome.
3. Open multiple extracted MMD/DAT files together.
   Overview can also extract/import editable members from/to your own AFS files.

No installation, Python, Node.js, emulator or internet is required.
Keep js and assets beside index.html. Original inputs are never overwritten.
Export your edits before importing the saved files into a new patched AFS copy.
